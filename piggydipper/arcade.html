Welcome to piggydipper.com

Meet Jana

Hi, I'm Jana, owner and operator of Piggy Dipper Cake Pops.

I haven't always been a baker. In fact, the very first cake pop I ever made was just this year. But I've always been fascinated by the art of baking and the creativity that goes into transforming simple ingredients into something both beautiful and delicious.

Like many people, I wear a lot of hats. I'm a wife, a mother of two, and a nurse. Through all of life's busy moments, I've always appreciated how food and desserts bring people together and help create lasting memories.

What started as curiosity quickly grew into a passion. I love experimenting with flavors, colors, and designs, and finding new ways to create treats that are just as fun to look at as they are to eat.

At Piggy Dipper, every treat is made with care, attention to detail, and a commitment to quality. Whether it's a classic cake pop, a chocolate-dipped Oreo, a marshmallow, a pretzel stick, or one of our seasonal creations, my goal is simple: make something delicious that brings a smile to your face and a little extra sweetness to your day.

Inside the Piggy Dipper Kitchen

Every Piggy Dipper treat is handcrafted in our home kitchen in Redfield, Arkansas.

Our kitchen is more than just a place where treats are made—it's a space my husband and I built together. We completely remodeled it ourselves, from designing the layout to assembling and hanging the cabinets. We even poured and finished the concrete countertops by hand. Countless hours of planning, hard work, and attention to detail went into creating the kitchen that now serves as the heart of Piggy Dipper.

We take great pride in maintaining a clean, organized, and professional workspace. From ingredient preparation to packaging, every step is handled with care and attention to quality. We believe great treats start with a great environment, and we're proud of the kitchen where every Piggy Dipper creation comes to life.

When you purchase from Piggy Dipper, you're supporting a local family-owned business built on hard work, creativity, and a passion for sharing something sweet with others.

The Story Behind Piggy Dipper

Piggy Dipper started with a family joke.

Years ago, Jana's brother would jokingly call out "Piggyyyyy Dippp'N!" whenever someone wandered into the kitchen looking for a snack. Over time, the phrase became a running joke among family and friends whenever someone was caught enjoying a sweet treat.

When it came time to choose a name for the business, nothing felt more fitting.

Today, Piggy Dipper Cake Pops specializes in handcrafted cake pops and other chocolate-dipped treats, including Oreos, pretzel sticks, marshmallows, Rice Krispie treats, seasonal specialties, and our signature sampler, Poppy's Treat Trough.

The name may have started as a joke, but the mission is serious: creating delicious treats worth "Piggy Dip'N" for.

From birthdays and celebrations to everyday cravings, we're proud to make handcrafted sweets that bring people together, one dip at a time.
